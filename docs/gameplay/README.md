# Welcome to HyperEmpire

HyperEmpire turns trading on Hyperliquid into an empire builder. Every fee you pay on the Exchange earns **$aura**, and $aura grows your empire, starting with the Townhall. Next to it, the **Garden** is a cozy farming loop that runs on **$gold**: plant seeds, harvest crops, bake bread and make wine for the Farmers who work your fields.

{% hint style="info" %}
HyperEmpire is in early access. Growing times and some prices are **test values** so you can try a whole cycle in minutes; they will change before launch.
{% endhint %}

## What you can do today

| Building | What you do there | Currency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Exchange](exchange.md) | Trade Hyperliquid perps and earn a share of the daily $aura pool | $aura |
| [Townhall](progression/townhall.md) | Level up your empire; it caps and boosts every building's Mastery | $aura |
| [Garden](garden/README.md) | Buy seeds, plant, harvest, sell crops, cook in the Kitchen | $gold |
| [Tavern](villagers/farmers.md) | Recruit Farmers to work your Garden, and revive fallen ones | $aura |

The Marketplace, Workshop, Mine and Leaderboard are in town but not open yet. See [Coming soon](coming-soon.md).
