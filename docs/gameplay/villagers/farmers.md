# Farmers

Farmers are villagers who work your Garden. Up to **3** can work at once. Each one adds a **yield** bonus (more crops at harvest) and a **growth** bonus (shorter growing time).

## Recruiting

Open the **Tavern** in town and press **RECRUIT**. A recruit costs **1,000 $aura** (burned) and rolls a random grade. Then it rolls its two perks, each a whole percent within that grade's range.

| Grade | Chance | Yield bonus | Growth bonus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Common | 70% | +1–5% | −1–4% |
| Uncommon | 22% | +4–10% | −3–8% |
| Rare | 6.5% | +8–18% | −6–12% |
| Epic | 1.4% | +15–30% | −10–18% |
| Legendary | 0.1% | +25–50% | −15–25% |

The Tavern lists every Farmer you have, where it is (in the Garden or resting), and how fed and happy it is.

## Putting Farmers to work

In the Garden, the **FARMERS** panel has 3 slots:

* Click **+ ADD A FARMER** and pick a resting Farmer to put it to work.
* Click **✕** on a slot to send that Farmer back to rest.

The panel header shows your working Farmers' total bonus. Their perks add up, and the growth cut is capped at **−60%** however many good Farmers you have. Working Farmers walk the road around the field; a Farmer above common has an outline in its grade's colour.

For when each bonus applies, see [Growing and harvesting](../garden/growing.md#what-farmers-change).

## Looking after them

Working Farmers get **hungry** and lose **happiness**. Resting Farmers don't. A hungry Farmer eats bread and an unhappy one drinks wine:

* An unhappy Farmer gives only half its perks.
* A Farmer that starves dies until you revive it with $aura.

See [Hunger and happiness](hunger-and-happiness.md).
