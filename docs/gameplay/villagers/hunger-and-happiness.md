# Hunger and happiness

Villagers are people. While they work they get hungry and lose happiness. **Resting villagers don't change**, so a Farmer sitting in the Tavern costs you nothing.

Each Farmer's slot in the Garden shows two bars, **hunger** (with a bread icon) and **happiness** (with a wine icon). Each slot has a **FEED** button (gives one bread) and a **WINE** button (gives one wine).

| | Hunger | Happiness |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full to empty, while working | **3 days** | **6 days** |
| Restored by | <img src="../.gitbook/assets/bread.png" alt="" width="24"> 1 bread: a third (1 day) | <img src="../.gitbook/assets/wine.png" alt="" width="24"> 1 wine: half (3 days) |
| A working villager needs | **1 bread a day** | **1 wine every 3 days** |
| At 0 | **Dies** | **Miserable:** no perks, but it doesn't die |

Neither bar goes above full, so you can feed up to 3 days of bread and 2 wines ahead. Make bread and wine in the [Kitchen](../garden/kitchen.md).

## Hunger

The slot shows how long the food lasts ("Food for 2d 4h"). Under a day it turns red and says **Hungry!**

When hunger reaches 0, the Farmer **dies**:

* It keeps its slot but adds no bonus and stops walking the road.
* It can't be fed or put back to work until you revive it.

{% hint style="warning" %}
A dead Farmer's bonus is gone until it's revived. If one dies just before you harvest, that harvest gets no yield bonus from it.
{% endhint %}

### Reviving

Press **REVIVE** on the Farmer's slot in the Garden, or on its card in the Tavern. It costs $aura (burned) by grade:

| Grade | Revive cost | Recruiting another of that grade takes, on average |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common | 250 $aura | about 1,400 $aura |
| Uncommon | 500 $aura | about 4,500 $aura |
| Rare | 1,000 $aura | about 15,000 $aura |
| Epic | 2,500 $aura | about 71,000 $aura |
| Legendary | 10,000 $aura | about 1,000,000 $aura |

A revived Farmer comes back with **1 day of food** and **happiness 50**, so feed it soon.

## Happiness and mood

Happiness sets a villager's mood, and the mood sets how much of its perks count:

| Mood | Happiness | Perks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Happy** | a third (33) or more | Full |
| **Unhappy** | under 33 | Half: a +9% Farmer gives +4.5% |
| **Miserable** | 0 | None |

The slot shows the mood under the bar, and the perks as they count right now ("+4.5% yield · −3% growth (half perks)").

A single wine (+50) always makes even a miserable Farmer Happy. Give one every 3 days and it stays between 50 and 100, always Happy.

{% hint style="info" %}
Mood counts at the same moments as the perks: when you plant (growth) and when you harvest (yield).
{% endhint %}

## What it costs

With 3 working Farmers, upkeep is 3 bread and 1 wine a day:

| Per day | Takes | Worth at market prices |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 bread | about 47 wheat | about 14 $gold |
| 1 wine | about 13 grapes | about 7.6 $gold |

That's about a third of what a casual Garden makes in a day. See [Your first day](../guides/first-day.md#is-it-worth-it) for the full numbers.
