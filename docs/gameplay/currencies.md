# $aura and $gold

HyperEmpire has two currencies with different jobs. **$aura** is scarce and earned by trading; it pays for power and status. **$gold** is earned by playing; it pays for upkeep, so you never burn $aura just to keep your empire running.

| | $aura | $gold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Earned by | Trading on the [Exchange](exchange.md) (a share of the daily pool) | Selling crops to the [Garden](garden/selling.md) market |
| Pays for | [Townhall](progression/townhall.md) levels, [Mastery](progression/mastery.md) respecs, recruiting and reviving [Farmers](villagers/farmers.md) | Seed packs |
| Starting amount | 5,000 | 0 (testnet: free +1,000 button) |
| Can you send it to others? | No | No |

## $aura

$aura comes from trading. Each day a fixed pool of 100,000 $aura is shared by everyone who traded through the Exchange, by the fees they paid. See [Exchange](exchange.md#earning-aura).

When you spend $aura it's **burned**: it leaves the game for good. That keeps $aura scarce.

## $gold

$gold comes from playing the Garden. You spend it on seeds, grow crops and sell the harvest back to the market for a little more than the seeds cost. See [Selling crops](garden/selling.md).

{% hint style="info" %}
Nothing you buy with $gold ever creates $aura. The only way to get $aura is trading.
{% endhint %}

Later, the [Marketplace](coming-soon.md#marketplace) will let players trade $gold and crops with each other for $aura.
