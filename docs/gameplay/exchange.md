# Exchange

The Exchange is a full trading screen for **Hyperliquid perpetuals**. Your orders go to Hyperliquid itself; HyperEmpire adds a small builder fee, and that fee is what earns you $aura.

## Setting up

The first time you trade, the Exchange asks for two one-time signatures. Neither costs gas.

1. **Approve the builder fee.** This lets HyperEmpire add its fee to your orders (up to Hyperliquid's 0.1% cap; we charge 0.05%).
2. **Approve a trading key.** A key kept in your browser places orders for you, so you don't sign every trade. It can trade, but it can **never withdraw** your funds.

## Trading

Market and limit orders, take-profit and stop-loss, leverage, and every Hyperliquid perps market are open from the start. Nothing is locked behind levels. Your positions, open orders and fills show under the chart, with the $aura each fill earned.

## Earning $aura

Every UTC day, a pool of **100,000 $aura** is shared by everyone who traded through the Exchange that day, by the builder fees they paid. Opens and closes both count, win or lose.

> **Your share** = 100,000 × your builder fees that day ÷ everyone's builder fees that day

| On a day when everyone pays… | …your $20 of fees earns |
| --- | --- |
| $200 in total | 10% of the pool: **10,000 $aura** |
| $2,000 in total | 1% of the pool: **1,000 $aura** |

So $aura per dollar of fees is higher on quiet days and lower on busy ones. Until a day settles, your share shows as **pending $aura**. It's added to your balance once the day ends and its fees are confirmed.

{% hint style="info" %}
Trading more than you otherwise would rarely pays. Every dollar of fees you add also grows everyone's total, and the pool stays the same size.
{% endhint %}

## Exchange Mastery

Trading also raises your **Exchange Mastery**: 100 XP for every $1 of builder fees. Its perks lower your fee and raise your weight in the daily pool. Open the **MASTERY** tab on the Exchange. See [Mastery](progression/mastery.md#exchange-mastery).
