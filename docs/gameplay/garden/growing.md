# Growing and harvesting

## Planting

1. In the **SEEDS** tab, click a seed to pick it. A bar appears showing how long it takes to ripen and how much it yields, with your Farmers' bonus included.
2. Press **PLANT 1**, or **PLANT X** to fill X empty plots at once. You can also click an empty plot to plant the picked seed there.

Click the picked seed again to put it down.

## Growing

A plant grows on the clock through three stages (sprout, young, ripe), evenly spaced over its growing time. It needs no watering or care. You don't need to stay on the screen; plants keep growing while you're away.

## Harvesting

Click a **ripe** plant to harvest it. Its crops go into the **HARVEST** tab, a "+N" floats up from the plot, and the plot is empty again.

> **Crops from one plant** = the seed grade's yield × (1 + your Farmers' yield bonus), rounded

## What Farmers change

[Farmers](../villagers/farmers.md) working the Garden add two bonuses, and each one applies at a different moment:

| Bonus | When it applies | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Growth** (−% growing time) | When you **plant** | It's fixed when the seed goes in. Adding or removing a Farmer later doesn't change crops already in the ground |
| **Yield** (+% crops) | When you **harvest** | It uses the Farmers working at that moment. Put your best Farmers to work before you harvest |

The bonus counts only from living Farmers, and an unhappy Farmer gives half. See [Hunger and happiness](../villagers/hunger-and-happiness.md).

**Example:** three Farmers with +21% yield and −13% growth make wheat ripen in about 2 minutes 37 seconds instead of 3, and a common plant give 36 wheat instead of 30.
