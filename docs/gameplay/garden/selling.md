# Selling crops

In the **HARVEST** tab, click a crop to pick it. A bar shows the price per crop, with two buttons:

* **SELL 10** sells 10.
* **SELL ALL** sells every one you hold.

Each button shows the $gold it pays. A sale pays the total **rounded down** to whole $gold.

| Crop | Price each | A common plant sells for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <img src="../.gitbook/assets/wheat.png" alt="" width="32"> Wheat | about 0.30 $gold | about 9 $gold (30 wheat) |
| <img src="../.gitbook/assets/grapes.png" alt="" width="32"> Grapes | about 0.57 $gold | about 22 $gold (40 grapes) |

## Is it profitable?

Yes, but only a little: that's on purpose.

* **On average, a pack's crops sell for 1.1× what the pack cost.** Lucky grades make up the difference.
* **A pack of only common seeds sells for a bit less than it cost.** For example, 300 wheat sells for about 91 $gold against the pack's 100.
* **Farmers' yield bonus is extra profit on top.** It's the reward for the $aura you spent recruiting them.

{% hint style="info" %}
Prices are set from the seed price and the grade odds, so selling to the market can never print $gold faster than seeds cost.
{% endhint %}

Later you'll also be able to sell crops to other players on the [Marketplace](../coming-soon.md#marketplace).
