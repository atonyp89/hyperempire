# The Garden

The Garden is HyperEmpire's cozy loop. Click the Garden in town to open its screen: a farmyard with a fenced field of **16 plots** in the middle, and a road around it where your working [Farmers](../villagers/farmers.md) stroll.

## The loop

1. **Buy a seed pack** with $gold. It opens straight away into 10 seeds of random grades. See [Seeds and grades](seeds.md).
2. **Plant** the seeds in empty plots. See [Growing and harvesting](growing.md).
3. **Wait** while they grow: sprout, then young plant, then ripe.
4. **Harvest** ripe plants into your inventory.
5. **Sell** crops to the market for $gold ([Selling crops](selling.md)), or **cook** them in the [Kitchen](kitchen.md) into food and wine for your Farmers.

## The inventory

Beside the field, your inventory has three tabs:

| Tab | Holds | Click an item to… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **SEEDS** | Seed packs and seeds, plus the **BUY SEED PACK** shop | Open a pack, or pick a seed to plant |
| **HARVEST** | Crops | Pick a crop to sell |
| **KITCHEN** | Flour, bread and wine, with the cooking steps | — |

Each tab shows how many items it holds. Better seeds get a coloured outline in their grade's colour.
