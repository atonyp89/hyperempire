# Seeds and grades

## Seed packs

Seeds come in packs of **10**. Open the shop with **BUY SEED PACK** in the Seeds tab, pick a pack and press **BUY**. The pack opens straight away and shows you the 10 seeds it gave. Click anywhere to close.

| Pack | Price | Seeds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <img src="../.gitbook/assets/wheat-pack.png" alt="" width="32"> Wheat seed pack | 100 $gold | 10 wheat seeds |
| <img src="../.gitbook/assets/grapes-pack.png" alt="" width="32"> Grape seed pack | 250 $gold | 10 grape seeds |

## Grades

Every seed rolls its own grade when the pack opens. A better grade yields more at harvest.

| Grade | Chance | Yield | Wheat per plant | Grapes per plant |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Common | 70% | ×1 | 30 | 40 |
| Uncommon | 22% | ×1.5 | 45 | 60 |
| Rare | 6.5% | ×2 | 60 | 80 |
| Epic | 1.4% | ×3 | 90 | 120 |
| Legendary | 0.1% | ×5 | 150 | 200 |

On average a seed yields **×1.21** of a common one, about 36 wheat or 48 grapes.

**How to spot them:**

* Seeds of the same crop and grade stack in one inventory slot.
* A better seed's icon gets an outline in its grade's colour: green (uncommon), blue (rare), purple (epic) or gold (legendary, which also glows).
* A planted plot shows a small gem in its grade's colour.
