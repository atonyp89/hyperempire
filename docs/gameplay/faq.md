# FAQ

<details>

<summary>Does signing in cost gas?</summary>

No. Signing in only signs a message. The Exchange's two setup approvals are signatures too, with no gas.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can the Exchange's trading key withdraw my funds?</summary>

No. It can place and cancel orders, never withdraw.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why did I earn less $aura today than yesterday for the same trading?</summary>

The daily pool is fixed at 100,000 $aura and shared by everyone's fees. On a busier day your share is smaller. See [Exchange](exchange.md#earning-aura).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Do my crops keep growing while I'm away?</summary>

Yes. Plants grow on the clock, so come back and harvest whenever you like. Ripe plants wait for you.

</details>

<details>

<summary>I put a better Farmer to work. Why didn't my crops speed up?</summary>

The growth bonus is fixed when a seed is planted. Your next plantings get the new bonus. The yield bonus is different: it applies at harvest, so it counts straight away. See [Growing and harvesting](garden/growing.md#what-farmers-change).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Do resting Farmers need food?</summary>

No. Only working Farmers get hungry or lose happiness. Resting ones stay exactly as they were.

</details>

<details>

<summary>My Farmer died. Is it gone?</summary>

No. Revive it for $aura (250 for a Common, up to 10,000 for a Legendary) from its Garden slot or in the Tavern. See [Hunger and happiness](villagers/hunger-and-happiness.md#reviving).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can an unhappy Farmer die?</summary>

No. Low happiness only shrinks its perks: half when unhappy, none when miserable. Only hunger can kill.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why do testnet and mainnet show different progress?</summary>

They're separate empires with separate balances. Testnet $aura and $gold have no value.

</details>
