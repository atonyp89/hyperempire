# Getting started

## Connect and sign in

1. Press **CONNECT WALLET** in the top-right corner and pick your wallet.
2. Open any building. The first time, it asks you to **SIGN IN**: your wallet signs a short message to prove the address is yours.

{% hint style="success" %}
Signing in is free. It sends no transaction and costs no gas.
{% endhint %}

Your empire is created the first time you sign in, with **5,000 starter $aura** and a level 1 Townhall.

## Testnet and mainnet

The header has a **TESTNET / MAINNET** switch. Each network is a separate empire with its own balances and progress. Testnet $aura and $gold have no value, so it's the place to learn the game.

On testnet, the **+1,000** button next to your $gold balance gives you free $gold to try the Garden.

## The town

The main screen is your town, seen from above. Your knight walks around it. Hover a building to see what it does, and click it to open its screen.

| Building | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Townhall | Open |
| Exchange | Open |
| Garden | Open |
| Tavern | Open (Farmers) |
| Marketplace | Coming soon |
| Workshop | Coming soon |
| Mine | Coming soon |

The town follows your clock: it gets dark at night and the lights come on. The time chip in the header shows the time of day, marked **AUTO**. Click it to preview morning, evening or night, and keep clicking to get back to **AUTO**.

## The header

| Item | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| $AURA | Your $aura balance. See [$aura and $gold](currencies.md) |
| $GOLD | Your $gold balance, with the testnet **+1,000** button |
| Address | The wallet you're signed in with |
