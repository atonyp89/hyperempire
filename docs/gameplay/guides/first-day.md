# Your first day

This walks you through the whole Garden loop once. It's easiest on **testnet**, where $gold is free.

## 1. Get some $gold

On testnet, press **+1,000** next to your $gold in the header.

## 2. Buy and plant wheat

1. Open the **Garden** and press **BUY SEED PACK** in the Seeds tab.
2. Buy a **Wheat seed pack** (100 $gold). It opens into 10 seeds; look for a lucky grade.
3. Click a seed, then **PLANT X** to fill the empty plots.

## 3. Recruit a Farmer (optional)

Open the **Tavern** in town and **RECRUIT** a Farmer for 1,000 $aura from your starting 5,000. Back in the Garden, click **+ ADD A FARMER** to put it to work. Its yield bonus applies to everything you harvest from now on. Its growth bonus applies to what you plant next. See [Farmers](../villagers/farmers.md).

## 4. Harvest

When a plant is ripe, click it. The wheat lands in the **HARVEST** tab.

## 5. Sell or cook

* **Sell:** pick the wheat in the Harvest tab and press **SELL ALL** for $gold.
* **Cook:** if you have Farmers working, keep enough wheat to **Mill** and **Bake** some bread in the Kitchen tab, then press **FEED** on each Farmer.

Grow some grapes too (a Grape seed pack is 250 $gold) and **Ferment** wine, so your Farmers stay happy. See [Kitchen](../garden/kitchen.md).

## 6. Come back

Replant whenever your plots are empty. Keep each working Farmer fed with about 1 bread a day, and give it 1 wine every 3 days.

## Is it worth it?

Say you replant all 16 plots with wheat twice a day and have 3 common Farmers working:

| Per day | $gold |
| --- | --- |
| 32 seeds (3.2 packs) | −320 |
| Selling the harvest (about 1,160 wheat) | +352 |
| Farmers' yield bonus (+3% each) | +32 |
| **Profit before upkeep** | **about 64** |
| 3 bread (about 47 wheat) | −14 |
| 1 wine (about 13 grapes) | −7.6 |
| **Profit after upkeep** | **about 42** |

Upkeep is about a third of what the Garden earns, so looking after your Farmers always pays. If you only replant once a day you still come out ahead, though by less (about 10 $gold a day).

{% hint style="info" %}
Unless you replant the moment crops ripen, how often you check in sets your pace, not the growing time. So the yield bonus is the Farmer perk that matters most.
{% endhint %}
