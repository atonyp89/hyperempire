# Townhall

The Townhall is your empire's main level. Raising it:

* **Caps every building's Mastery:** no Mastery can be higher than your Townhall level.
* **Speeds up Mastery everywhere:** +1% Mastery XP in every building per Townhall level.
* **Will rank you on the Leaderboard** when it opens (see [Coming soon](../coming-soon.md#leaderboard)).

## Level cost

Going from level L to L + 1 costs **1,000 × (L + 1)^1.6 $aura**, burned.

| Upgrade | Cost | Total $aura from level 1 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 → 2 | 3,031 | 3,031 |
| 4 → 5 | 13,133 | 31,154 |
| 9 → 10 | 39,811 | 172,538 |
| 19 → 20 | 120,684 | 988,464 |
| 49 → 50 | 522,820 | 10,316,015 |

## Risky upgrades

You don't have to pay the full price. Pick a chance from **10% to 100%** (in steps of 10%) and pay that share of the cost:

* At 100% the level is guaranteed.
* At 50% you pay half the cost and succeed half the time.
* The $aura burns **whether or not** the upgrade succeeds.

Over many tries a risky upgrade costs the same as paying in full on average, so choose by how lucky you feel.

{% hint style="warning" %}
A failed attempt is gone. If you can only just afford a level, paying in full is the safe choice.
{% endhint %}
