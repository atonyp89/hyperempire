# Coming soon

These are planned. Details may change before they open.

## Marketplace

Trade **$gold, crops and later every item** with other players for **$aura**. Sellers pay a 10% tax. There's no Mastery and no rewards for trading here.

## Mine

Send Miners to dig. Every minute the Mine produces a block of rewards, shared by mining power. Miners will eat the same [bread](garden/kitchen.md) as Farmers.

## Workshop

Craft Picks for Miners and repair worn ones; gear crafting later.

## Leaderboard

Season 0 will rank empires by **Townhall level**.

## More Mastery

[Mastery](progression/mastery.md) trees for the Garden (faster growth, more plots), Tavern, Workshop and Mine.

## Heroes

More Tavern recruits: heroes with stats who go on adventures.
